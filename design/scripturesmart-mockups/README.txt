ScriptureSmart Approved UI Mockups

01-home.png
Home screen target. Use this as the visual foundation for the application shell, simplified navigation, hero, AI/search bar, pathway cards, recent content, and brand system.

02-study-desk.png
Study Desk target. Scripture is the visual center. Left = resources/tools. Center = passage reading and expandable study areas. Right = persistent interactive ScriptureSmart AI.

03-explore.png
Explore / Resource Center target. Treat ScriptureSmart as a rich theological library: search, filters, Scripture, historical commentary, early church, councils/creeds, modern voices, sermons, church resources, and personal library.

04-sermon-builder.png
Create / Sermon Builder target. Premium writing studio with structured sermon creation, research/inspiration, and persistent ScriptureSmart AI assistance.

05-group.png
Group page target. Warm community workspace with group identity, next meeting, current study, weekly discussion, prayer requests, shared resources, and church-sourced content such as sermon series and events. Existing meal planning and kids planning must also remain available in the final group experience even though they are not fully shown in this mockup.

IMPLEMENTATION RULE:
These are target screens, not loose inspiration. The frontend should be substantially rewritten to closely match their layout, hierarchy, density, typography, visual warmth, and interaction patterns.

PRESERVE EXISTING SYSTEMS:
- YouVersion / Bible APIs
- ScriptureSmart AI backend and interactions
- AI review of notes
- Authentication
- Persistence/database
- Sermons/studies
- Groups and The Table
- Meal planning
- Kids/childcare planning
- Prayer requests
- Provider/security boundaries
- Existing source-integrity rules

Do not replace working integrations just to match the visuals. Rebuild the presentation layer around the existing application logic.
